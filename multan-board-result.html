Frequently asked questions

How do I check my result on BISE Multan?

Select your class on this page, enter your roll number, then click “Check Result”. You'll be redirected to the official BISE Multan website to view your result.

Which districts does BISE Multan cover?

Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education, Multan conducts exams and publishes results for students in Multan, Khanewal, Lodhran and Vehari.

What is the official website of BISE Multan?

The official website is https://bisemultan.edu.pk/. Always double-check the address bar to make sure you're on the genuine Board site before entering personal details.

Can I check results for more than one class on this Board?

Yes. BISE Multan publishes separate results for 9th, 10th, 11th and 12th Class. Use the links on this page to jump to the class you need.
