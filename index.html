How to check your Punjab Board result

The same four steps work for every class and every Board.

  1. Select your class and Board

    Use the form above or pick a class/Board page from the lists on this site.

  2. Click “Check Result”

    You will be taken to that Board's official website in a new tab.

  3. Enter your roll number on the official site

    Type the roll number exactly as printed on your admit card / roll number slip.

  4. View and download your result

    Save or print the result card directly from the Board's own website for your records.

Why we don't show results directly

Result data belongs to the Board that conducted the exam. Third-party copies can go out of date or be wrong. Sending you to the source keeps your result accurate and official.

Frequently asked questions

Does Punjab Result Portal show my result directly on this site?

No. This site never displays, stores or hosts result data. When you click “Check Result” you are redirected to the official website of the Board you selected, and your result is shown there.

Which Boards are covered?

All nine Punjab examination boards are covered: Lahore, Gujranwala, Faisalabad, Rawalpindi, Multan, Sargodha, Sahiwal, Bahawalpur and DG Khan.

Is my roll number saved anywhere?

No. We do not collect, transmit to our own servers, or store the roll number you type. It is only used locally in your browser to help you get to the right official page.

What do I do if the official Board website is slow or down?

Board websites can get busy right after a result is announced. Wait a few minutes and try again, or check the result through your school, as schools usually also receive result gazettes.

Is this an official Government website?

No. Punjab Result Portal is an independent, privately run information website and is not affiliated with any Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education or any Government department.
