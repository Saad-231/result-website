Frequently asked questions

How can I check my 10th Class result 2026?

Select “10th Class” and your Board on this page, then click “Check Result”. You will be redirected to your Board's official website, where you enter your roll number to view the result.

When will the 10th Class result 2026 be announced?

The expected date shown above is provisional and set by the Board. Always confirm the exact date and time from your Board's official website closer to the announcement.

Can I check my result without a roll number?

Most Board websites require a roll number. If you've lost your roll number slip, contact your school or the Board's help desk to recover it.

Do I need my 9th Class marks for the 10th Class result?

Yes. The final SSC result is typically a combination of your 9th Class (Part-I) and 10th Class (Part-II) marks, calculated by the Board.

Is there a fee to check my result on this site?

No. Punjab Result Portal is free to use. We only link you to the Board's own website, where checking your result is also free.
