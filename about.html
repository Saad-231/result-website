About Punjab Result Portal

A simple, independent starting point for finding the correct official Board result website.

What this website does

Punjab Result Portal helps students quickly identify which official Board website to visit for their 9th, 10th, 11th or 12th Class result. You select your class and Board, and we send you straight to that Board's own result page.

What this website does not do

We do not fetch, scrape, store, publish or guarantee any result data. We do not generate marksheets. We do not collect or store the roll numbers students type into our search form. Every result shown to a student comes from that Board's own official website, on that Board's own domain.

Why we built it

On result day, official Board websites can be hard to find or easy to confuse with look-alike sites. This portal keeps one clean, fast list of the genuine Board websites, organised by class and by Board, so students spend less time searching and more time on the real result page.
